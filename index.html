Your whole Google Drive, on your own hard drive.

DriveSync Pro is a free Windows app that copies files between Google Drive and your PC or external drive, and picks up where it left off.

Download

Copy any Drive folder, or your whole Drive, to a folder or external disk.

Upload

Send a folder from your PC to Google Drive, with every file checked after upload.

Pause anytime

Stop, unplug or restart. Files you already have are skipped next time.

Runs on your PC

Your files travel only between your computer and Google. Nothing passes through our servers.

How it uses your Google account

When you click Connect Google Drive, Google asks you to let DriveSync Pro see, edit, create and delete files in your Google Drive. The app needs this to download the folders you pick and upload the folders you choose. It only acts when you start a transfer, and the sign-in is stored on your own PC. Read the privacy policy for details.

Install

Run DriveSyncPro-Setup.exe and click Install. No administrator rights are needed. Works on Windows 10 and 11.
